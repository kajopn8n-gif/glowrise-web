Say which iPhone you have and what iOS version it runs — it is almost always the first thing worth knowing.

The alarm did not ring

Glow Rise alarms need iOS 26.1 or newer, and permission to schedule alarms. If the phone never asked, open Settings › Glow Rise and check that alarms are allowed. An alarm also cannot ring on a phone that is switched off or out of battery, so for anything that truly matters, set a second alarm on another device.

Where is my data

On your phone, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server, which also means there is no backup: a lost or wiped phone takes the journal with it. Everything about this is in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting everything

Me › Your data › Erase everything wipes it permanently. Deleting the app does the same.
