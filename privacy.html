Glow Rise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Glow Rise is built so that there is almost nothing to write a privacy policy about. Your alarms, your journal, your cycle and everything else you type stays on this phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no Glow Rise server anywhere for it to be sent to. This policy explains that in full, and is honest about the one part that does involve somebody else: the ads in the free version.

1. Who is responsible

Glow Rise is made and published by an individual developer, reachable at kajopn8n@gmail.com. Write with any question about this policy, or to ask anything about the data on your phone.

2. What Glow Rise stores, and where

Everything you put into the app is written to the private storage of this app on this phone, which no other app can read:

  • the name you choose to be greeted by;
  • your alarms — times, repeat days, notes, and which wake-up missions you picked;
  • your journal entries and the mood you tapped with each one;
  • the days you marked as period days, and whether you took your contraception;
  • your morning routine ticks and streak;
  • whether this phone has Glow Rise Premium.

None of it is uploaded, backed up to us, shared, sold, or seen by anybody but you. We have no copy of it and no way to get one.

3. Health data

Period days and contraception reminders are health information, and the most sensitive thing in the app. They are treated exactly like everything else above: stored on the phone, never sent anywhere. Glow Rise does not read from or write to Apple Health or Google Health Connect.

What the Cycle tab shows is arithmetic on the dates you entered. It is an estimate, not a diagnosis, and it is not contraception. Do not use Glow Rise to decide whether you can get pregnant.

4. Advertising

The free version of Glow Rise shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, and a full-screen ad after a finished wake-up or a saved journal entry.

To choose an ad, Google receives technical information from your phone — an advertising identifier, rough location derived from your network, device type and language. It never receives anything you wrote in Glow Rise: not your journal, not your cycle, not your alarms, not your name.

On iPhone you are asked first whether Glow Rise may track you across other apps and websites. Say no and the ads become non-personalised; nothing else about the app changes. You can change that answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google explains what it does with ad requests at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Glow Rise Premium removes every ad, and with it this entire section.

5. Purchases

Glow Rise Premium is bought through Apple on iPhone and through Google on Android. The payment happens entirely inside their systems: they tell the app only whether this phone has paid. We never see your card, your name, your address, or your purchase history.

6. Notifications

Glow Rise asks permission to put alarms and reminders on your phone. That permission is used for nothing else — no marketing, no announcements. Refuse it and the rest of the app still works; only the ringing stops.

7. What we do not do

No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking pixels, no advertising profile built by us, no e-mail list, no social network logins, and no selling or sharing of personal data with anybody. There is nothing to sell: we do not have your data.

8. Your rights, and how to use them

Because everything lives on your phone, you exercise your rights directly, without asking us:

  • to see your data — open the app;
  • to correct it — edit or delete the entry;
  • to erase all of it — Me › Your data › Erase everything, which wipes it permanently, or delete the app, which does the same.

Under the GDPR you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw.

9. How long it is kept

For as long as you keep it. Glow Rise deletes nothing by itself and expires nothing. Erasing it is always your decision, and it is immediate.

10. Children

Glow Rise is not meant for children under 13, and is not directed at them. We cannot knowingly collect anything from a child because we do not collect anything from anybody.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version appears in the app in the same update. Changes that would mean collecting something new will be said plainly, not hidden in a rewrite.
